Sample report

This is a shortened example of the 2027 Year Ahead + Love Reading, written for a made-up person. Yours is written from your own four pillars.

Day Master · Yang Wood 甲

The Great Tree

You are the tall pine: steady, honest and slow to bend. People lean on you because you don't move when the wind does. Your growth edge is letting yourself rest without feeling you have to earn it.

Your five-element balance

Wood 38% · Water 25% · Earth 19% · Fire 12% · Metal 6%

Plenty of Wood and Water (roots and rain), light on Metal. In tradition, Metal is the pruning shears: structure, deadlines and saying no. Years that bring Metal ask you to trim what isn't growing.

2027 · 丁未 Fire Goat year

For a Wood Day Master, Yin Fire is your Hurting Officer: expression, new ideas and pushing back on old rules. Expect 2027 to pull you toward creating, teaching or starting something of your own. The Goat's Earth is your Wealth, so effort you put out this year is read as turning into money more directly than usual.

Watch for: speaking before thinking with bosses or family. Fire burns Wood when there's too much of it.

Month by month (excerpt)

Feb–Mar 2027 (Tiger month): your own Wood is strong. A good start to set one clear goal.

Jun–Jul 2027 (Horse month): Fire peaks. Great for visibility and launches; plan rest so you don't burn out.

Aug–Sep 2027 (Monkey month): Metal, your Officer, arrives. Tradition reads structure and decisions: a new role, a deadline or a clear yes or no.

Love

For a woman with this chart, the partner star is Metal; for a man, it's Earth. With Earth carried all year by the Goat, tradition reads 2027 as an active year for commitment, especially late summer.

Try: say what you want out loud instead of waiting to be asked. The Great Tree gives shade; let someone give some back.

One question to carry

What would I build this year if I didn't need anyone's permission?
