Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Draft, to be reviewed before scaling.

What we collect

Only what your reading needs: birth date, birth time (optional), birth city and gender (optional). Your free chart is calculated on your own device and is not sent to us.

If you join the waitlist we store your email and your Day Master. If you buy a reading we store your email, your chart and the reading so we can deliver it and send you a copy. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number.

How we use it

To create and deliver your reading, to email you about your purchase, and (if you joined the waitlist) to tell you about our launch. We use basic, anonymous step counts to see how many people use each part of the site.

Who we share it with

Stripe (payments), our AI writing provider (your chart only, to write the reading; no email or name), and our email service. We don't sell your data.

Your choices

Email us to see or delete your data and we'll do it within 30 days. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
