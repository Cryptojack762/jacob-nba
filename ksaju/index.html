What is saju? Haneul explains

Saju sounds complicated, but it's really simple. Let me walk you through it in five small steps.
1

Your birth moment becomes four pillars

Saju (사주) means "four pillars". Koreans turn the moment you were born into four pillars: one for the year, one for the month, one for the day and one for the hour. Think of them as four snapshots of time, taken the moment you arrived.

2

Each pillar has two characters

Every pillar is written with two old characters. The top one comes from a set of 10, such as 甲 (a big tree) or 丙 (the sun). The bottom one is one of the 12 zodiac animals, like the Rat, Tiger or Rabbit. Four pillars × two characters = eight characters, which Koreans call your palja (팔자). Together, your saju palja (사주팔자).

3

One character is you

Of all eight, one matters most: the top character of your day pillar. It's called your Day Master (일간), and it describes your core personality. In the example it's 庚, The Sword: brave, direct and at their best when there's a real decision to make. There are 10 Day Masters, and you'll meet yours in a minute.

4

Five elements, one balance

Every character belongs to one of five elements, called ohaeng (오행): Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water. Most charts have a lot of some and none of others. The example has three Metal, three Wood and two Earth, but no Fire or Water.

Koreans read that balance like a recipe. What you have plenty of shows your natural strengths. What's missing shows what you tend to look for, in work and in the people you're drawn to.

5

Your chart moves through seasons

Your chart doesn't stay still. Every year brings a new element, and every ten years you enter a new "season of luck" (대운). That's why Koreans check their saju before a new year, a new job or a wedding. In a 2026 survey, 62.3% of Koreans said they had used fortune-telling services.

Today, most people use saju the way you might use your star sign: to understand yourself and your timing, not to predict your fate.

Questions

Is this fortune-telling?

Not the scary kind. Saju is a centuries-old Korean way of reading personality and timing from your birth moment. We use it for self-reflection and fun. It doesn't predict fixed outcomes. For entertainment and self-reflection only.

I don't know my birth time. Can I still do it?

Yes. Without the hour you still get six of your eight characters, including your Day Master. Your hour pillar just stays blank.

Why do you ask for my birth city?

We use true solar time for your birth city, as traditional Korean saju does. Clock time can be 30 minutes or more off the sun, which can change your chart.

I only know my lunar birthday.

Please convert it to the regular (solar) calendar first, then enter it. A family member or any online lunar-to-solar converter can help.

Why does my year start in February?

In saju, the new year begins at Ipchun (입춘), the start of spring, around Feb 4. If you were born in January or early February, your zodiac animal may be the previous year's.

Why do you ask for gender?

Only to set the direction of your 10-year luck cycles, which is how traditional saju works. You can skip it.

What happens to my birth details?

Your free chart is calculated on your phone. We only store your details if you buy a reading or join the list, and we never sell them.
