How to read Phoenix
ENTERSetup is live and the BTC gate allows it. Check the plan before any trade.
ALMOSTClose to the trigger line. Watch it.
WAITMid-range. Nothing to do yet.
AVOIDBelow its line or breaking down.
⚡BTC gate: when it's DOWN, bounce longs are a coin flip. Skip them or use a quarter size.
📏ATR = how far price sits from the trigger line, measured in average daily moves.
